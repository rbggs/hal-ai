EMPLOYEE VACATION POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Vacation Accrual
Full-time employees accrue vacation at the following rates based on years of service:
- 0 to 2 years: 10 days per year (0.833 days per month)
- 3 to 5 years: 15 days per year (1.25 days per month)
- 6 or more years: 20 days per year (1.667 days per month)

Part-time employees accrue vacation on a pro-rated basis relative to their scheduled hours.

Vacation Requests
Employees must submit vacation requests at least 5 business days in advance through the HR portal. Requests are subject to manager approval based on business needs. During peak periods (fiscal quarter-end, major product launches), blackout dates may apply and will be communicated 30 days in advance.

Vacation Carryover
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused vacation days into the next calendar year. Any accrued vacation beyond 5 days is forfeited on December 31. There is no payout for forfeited vacation.

Vacation Payout on Termination
Upon voluntary resignation or termination, employees will be paid out for all accrued and unused vacation days at their current base salary rate. This payout is included in the final paycheck.

Vacation During Notice Period
Employees may not take vacation during their notice period without prior written approval from HR. Approved vacation during notice period will be counted toward the notice period.

Sick Leave and Vacation
Vacation days may not be used in place of sick leave. Employees must use sick leave for illness-related absences. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out upon termination.

Contact
For questions about vacation policy, contact HR at hr@company.com or extension 4200.
